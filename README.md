Orbit & Mission Analysis: 
Derived Keplerian elements ($i=55^\circ$, $h=550\text{ km}$), modeled JGM-2 gravity field and solar/lunar perturbations in NASA GMAT, calculated a $37.2\%$ eclipse fraction, and selected ground stations for $>1.5\text{ hrs/day}$ contact time.
Space Environment & Survivability: Computed orbit-maintenance $\Delta v$ using the NRLMSIS atmospheric density model and executed an MMOD Whipple shield penetration probability assessment ($P \ge 1 \approx 29\%$) using NASA JSC-64399 ballistic limit equations.
Power & Subsystem Sizing: Sized lithium-ion battery capacity ($191\text{ Wh}$) and solar array area ($0.60\text{ m}^2$) based on mode-by-mode power loads (Spot/Swathe duty cycles).
Trade Studies & Mass Budget: Performed a propulsion trade study between Cold Gas ($\text{N}_2$) and Xenon Hall Thrusters, converging an iterated $\sim 120\text{ kg}$ spacecraft mass budget.
Launch Vehicle Compatibility & Structural Dynamics: Performed a 6-DOF lumped-mass stiffness matrix analysis to meet Electron’s fundamental axial frequency ($>30\text{ Hz}$) requirement, validating fairing dynamic envelope, mass margins, and mechanical separation constraints.
